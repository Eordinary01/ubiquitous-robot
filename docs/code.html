Operational Comparison

The problem, in plain words.

How gyms operate today

Workout routines are forwarded over WhatsApp messages, lost in chat histories, and forgotten by week two. Subscriptions and member renewal dates sit in physical paper notebooks at the front desk or in static, out-of-sync spreadsheets. Floor trainers answer the exact same diet and weight questions repeatedly because members have no single place to check their assigned plan.

STATUS: FRAGMENTED • UNTRACKED • TIME INTENSIVE
With FitConnect

Every member receives personal login access to your gym portal. Workouts, set prescriptions, target rep ranges, and macronutrient targets live in one structured dashboard updated by your staff. Membership terms, renewals, and onboarding requests are tracked cleanly without paper records or endless chat threads.

STATUS: UNIFIED • CENTRALIZED • REAL-TIME
Workflow Overview

How it works.

01 Intake

A member finds your gym and requests to join.

Prospective members open your unique gym URL or scan your front-desk QR code to register their details and select their desired membership category.

02 Provisioning

You approve in one click and a starter plan is created.

Alternatively, configure their membership duration, health info, baseline body measurements, and specific routine yourself through the administrative console.

03 Execution

They log in and see their workouts, diet, and progress.

Members sign in from any phone or browser to review daily prescribed training splits, nutrient counts, and update weight metrics after every gym session.

Administrative Control

What you manage.

Membership plans and status MODULE 01

Track active, expiring, and overdue memberships across your entire facility in real time. Renewals and term expirations update automatically without requiring manual ledger cross-checks.

FIELDS: DURATION, STATUS, EXPIRY
Weekly workout routines MODULE 02

Schedule routines from Monday through Saturday grouped by target muscle anatomy. Specify exact sets, rep ranges, target weights, and rest intervals per exercise.

FIELDS: MUSCLE, SETS, REPS, WEIGHT, REST
Diet plans MODULE 03

Prescribe precise daily meal breakdowns structured around clean macronutrient values. Total calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat targets display in unified tables.

FIELDS: MEAL, KCAL, PROT, CARB, FAT
Body measurements and progress MODULE 04

Record circumference readings, bodyweight records, and body composition figures across training cycles. Members and trainers view historical deltas to calibrate weekly workload adjustments.

FIELDS: WEIGHT, CHEST, WAIST, ARMS
Join requests MODULE 05

Filter incoming join submissions through a single, structured operational queue. Approve applications instantly or modify plan terms before provisioning access credentials.

FIELDS: APPLICANT, DATE, ACTION
